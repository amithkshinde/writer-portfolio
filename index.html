About

Hi, I'm Amith.

I'm a freelance content writer based in Bengaluru. I started out writing sports articles and match previews for a digital sports platform, covering cricket and other Indian sports, filing several pieces a week to editorial deadlines.

Since 2022 I've worked as a UX designer, and a big part of that job is writing. I wrote all the interface copy for products used by 100,000+ people: labels, error messages, empty states, onboarding. I also wrote research reports, design briefs and documentation for product managers and engineers.

Both jobs come down to the same thing. Work out who is reading, what they already know, and what they need next. Then write it plainly and get it in on time.

I write in English and work comfortably in Kannada and Hindi.
