We have seen the BJP making inroads successfully after the results of the Kerala Local Body Elections, the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation, the District Development Council in Jammu and Kashmir, among others. These were virtually impenetrable for a national party like BJP with the local parties having a significant ground presence and a keen understanding of the local caste politics and also having influence on the people. Even though the numbers that the BJP received in these elections are low, but breaking the barrier and getting inside was more important for the BJP.

The interim government of Mamata Banerjee has been accused of several allegation by the BJP, which is the main arch rival of the All India Trinamool Congress. One of the major allegations levelled by Amit Shah are that the TMC workers have killed more than 300 BJP workers in the recent past. This allegation was levelled post the attack on the convoy of BJP President JP Nadda.

There are severe allegations of misadministration of the government during the relief work post the Cyclone Amphan in May 2019 and also the state was not reporting the number of COVID-19 cases and the number of deaths of the same. Even the central health team was not entertained properly during their visit to the state considering the COVID-19. The Gorkhaland issue will also be playing a major role in the elections, the Gorkha Janmukti Morcha left the alliance with the BJP based on the Gorkhaland issue itself, and joined the Trinamool Congress.

These are certain issues which can affect the State Polls in 2021. Along with these the opposition can also pick up contentious issues like the mighty controversial farm bills which were brought about in haste without any consultations, the abrogation of article 370 and the current state of the citizens Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh, the stifle between Indian Army and Chinese PLA at the Galwan Valley and the resulting stand off, the Citizenship Amendment Act 2019 and the National Citizens Register are all going to play a major role in the shifting of power between the BJP and the opposition. We can already witness a lot of mudslinging in the state, we’ll need to wait and watch the people of the state will decide, as they say “The power of the people is always stronger than the people in power”.

Taking a look at the previous poll result that was conducted in the Summer of 2016, the Trinamool Congress led by Mamata Banerjee. The TMC could manage to gather 211 seats, the Indian National Congress came second behind by winning in 44 seats. The BJP could gather only 3 seats. Mamata Banerjee could form the government with the support of independent candidate. A host of close to fourteen parties which included parties like the CPI, CPM, RJD among others formed the opposition by forming the Mahajot (Left front – United Progressive Alliance). The National Democratic Alliance (NDA) was made up of the BJP and the Gorkha Janmukti Morcha (GJM). The Trinamool Congress were able to gather the highest number of popular votes, making 44.91%, followed by the CPM making 19.75%, the INC could gather 12.25% and the BJP 10.16%.

An opinion poll conducted by the ABP Group-CNX predicts that the NDA are going to gather some steam in this election and have some breakthrough, the NDA are predicted to be getting 32.74% and around 97-105 seats. The lead is going to be Trinamool Congress with close to 155-163 seats with a vote share of 38.50. We can see that according to the Opinion Polls that the AITC will be seen forming the government and the opposition will be led by the NDA.
