The Galaxy S23 Ultra is Samsung's top phone for 2023. It starts at $1,199, which is a lot of money. This is what you get for it, and what Samsung left the same.

Display

The screen is 6.8 inches, a Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel with QHD+ resolution. The refresh rate moves between 1Hz and 120Hz depending on what you are doing, and HDR10+ is supported. The edges are curved, and the curve can get in the way when you hold the phone or use the S Pen. The S Pen lives in a slot in the body.

It comes in Phantom Black, Green, Cream and Lavender.

Camera

The main camera is now 200MP. The other three on the back are a 12MP ultra-wide and two 10MP telephotos, one at 3x and one at 10x. You can shoot at 200MP, 50MP or 12MP. The higher the resolution, the bigger the file, so it helps to know which one you picked.

The front camera is 12MP. The S22 Ultra had 40MP, so this is a step down on paper.

Performance and battery

The chip is the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy. It is the regular Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 with a slightly higher clock speed on the main core, 3.36GHz against 3.2GHz. RAM is 8GB or 12GB, and storage goes from 256GB to 1TB.

The battery is 5,000mAh. Wired charging is still 45W, the same as before, and there is no charger in the box.

Software

It runs One UI 5.1 on Android 13. Samsung promises four years of Android updates and five years of security patches. The phone is IP68 rated, supports 5G and Wi-Fi 6E, and has an in-display fingerprint sensor.

What it is not

It is not cheap, and the charging speed did not improve. If you are on an S22 Ultra, the main changes are the 200MP camera and the new chip.
