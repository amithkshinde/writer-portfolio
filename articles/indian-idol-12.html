On the 12th of July, it was announced that the Indian Idol show will be making a comeback for the 12th season and the Auditions started on the online mode. Contestants were required to upload their singing videos and the judges would select them and call them to Mumbai for the main auditions.

The highly talented judges panel comprising of Vishal Dadlani, Himesh Reshammiya and Neha Kakkar will judge the contestants who have cleared the first round at the main auditions in Mumbai. We could see that out of 1000s of people who tried their hand at the competition, 32 were selected for the Theater Round.

The 32 contestants are Pawandeep Rajan, Shanmukha Priya, Sawai Bhatt, Anjali Gaikwad, Ashish Kulkarni, Sireesha Bhagavatula, Samyak Prasana, Vaishnav Girish, Mohd Danish, Yuvraj Medhe, Nihal Tauro, Arunita Kanjial, Sayli Kamble, Jeli Tamin, Sitara Parveen, Piyush Panwar, Anushka Banerjee, Riya Biswas, Shreya Basu, Karnoi Mega, Sahil Solanki, Nachiket Lele, Rasika Ganoo, Satyandar Arya, Arsh Mohammed, Akash Dubey, Chirag Kotwal, Tanishtha Puri, Nandini Gaikwad, Dipayan Banerjee, Adnan Hussain, and Deeksha Toor. Out of these 32 contestants four have received the coveted golden mic namely, Shanmukha Priya, Sawai Bhatt, Sayli Kamble and Anjali Gaikwad.

The contestants have been chosen from all over India and are all will be singing their best to clear the Theatre round and get themselves into the studios.

Among the contestants is Pawandeep Rajan impressed the judges in the main audition round, with his impressive singing skills and his hands on the grand piano. The promos show that he will now be working on the Guitar and impress the judges with a song from Rockstar. Sawai Bhatt, a poor puppeteer from Rajasthan left the judges awestruck with his impeccable singing skills which he acquired just from listening to songs on the radio. There is also a contestant named Yuvraj Medhe who used to work on the sets of the Indian Idol as a sweeper has now gotten himself selected for the theatre round of the auditions.

There will be total 16 slots in the Studio Round. The 32 contestants will be fighting tooth and nail and impress the judges. We need to wait and watch who will be able to make it to the studio round of the competition. Keep looking in this space for more updates on the competition.
