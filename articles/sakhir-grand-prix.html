Sakhir Grand Prix Preview and Predictions:

We have had the Bahrain GP and now it is time for the Sakhir Grand Prix. This Grand Prix takes place over 87 laps of the Bahrain International Circuit’s 3.543-kilometre Outer track layout on a December 6th Sunday. Post the horrific accident of Romain Grosjean in the Bahrain Grand Prix, Grosjean is set to be replaced by Brazilian racer Pietro Fittipaldi which is slated to be his debut race said Hass Team Principal Guenther Steiner. Also, Lewis Hamilton has tested COVID positive and will not be on the track.

The Drivers’ championship is done and dusted with Lewis Hamilton taking a record equalling 7th title in Turkey and the Constructors Championships was sealed long back by the Mercedes team. With this, the other teams will try to score as many brownie points as possible by going aggressive in terms of their strategy and upgrades for the upcoming races.

Sakhir Grand Prix Predictions and Odds: Championship Standings:

The championship seems to have slipped from other teams, with Lewis Hamilton leading with 332 points and standing far behind with 201 points is Valtteri Bottas and next comes Max Verstappen with 189 points. Although Hamilton isn’t racing this track due to COVID, the points gap between Bottas and Hamilton is too huge to be covered just in one race. Daniel Ricciardio is close behind with 102 points followed by Sergio Perez with 100 points.

Max Verstappen is trying to bridge the gap and make it to the 2nd spot. Sebastian Vettel is looking for another round of glary before leaving the Ferrari Haas is debuting its new driver Pietro Fittipaldi after the near-death accident of Romain Grosjean. George Russel and Nicholas Latifi are still looking forward to open their account.

Sakhir Grand Prix Preview and Predictions: Schedule:

The race week is scheduled to begin from 4th of December upto the race day on the 6th of December 2020. The two free-practices are scheduled on the 4th and 5th of December where the drivers run simulations and look at tyre compounds. Followed by the practice session is the qualifying where in the drivers will burn rubber to stay ahead of the others.

Sakhir Grand Prix Preview and Odds: History and Odds

This race will use the ‘Outer Circuit Layout’, the first time Formula One race will be held in this layout. This is shorter than the Grand Prix Layout which was 5.412km, this Grand Prix Layout was used for the Bahrain Grand Prix. The lap times are expected to be less than a minute, the only time sub-one-minute lap times have been achieved was at the 1974 French Grand Prix at Dijon-Prenois.

The seven-time world champion Lewis Hamilton has tested positive for the corona virus and is slated to miss the race as per the policies of Bahrain and thus we will need to see who is going to win the race this time.

The betting markets and prediction experts are expecting a win for Valtteri Bottas followed by Max Vertsappen and Sergio Perez.

888sport which is a premium betting platform, is expecting Bottas to get the top spot this race with a winning odds of 1.07, close on his heels is Vertsappen with an odds of 1.17.

Oddschecker puts the odds of Bottas winning at 5/1 and followed by Vertsappen at 6/1.
