The clock is ticking, as the throne is up for grabs in less than 2 months as the state of Karnataka is going to polls. As it has been the case in the last assembly election, the political watchdogs are expecting a hung assembly with BJP and INC not giving an inch, while JDS are hopeful of becoming the kingmaker. Given that the INC and JDS are a tough nut to crack in the Old Mysuru region, the BJP are looking to make the most in Bengaluru city, which has 28 assembly seats. On the other hand, the Congress and the Janata Dal are perturbed by their MLAs shifting to the BJP in 2019, and are keen on winning back their strongholds. With the battle shaping up quite well in the capital, let us look at how things are standing currently:

1. Yelahanka

This constituency is a typical illustration of a three-way battle where the Vokkaligas are the dominant caste with the Reddy community holding a significant chunk of votes. The incumbent MLA S R Vishwanath is a three-time winner, hoping for a fourth term while Congress and JDS are trying to play with the caste dynamics by fielding Keshava Rajanna and Munegowda respectively. This might just play into the hands of BJP as there is a big chance of vote split among the Vokkaligas with the Reddy community behind S R Vishwanath. Meanwhile, the previous runner-up, JDS are hopeful of a counter consolidation, with the accusation on the track record of the sitting MLA.

The BJP has held 3 out of 4 corporation wards in the constituency and are looking to extend their winning streak in 2023. Even though Munegowda is emerging as an able challenger to the incumbent after the latter failed to bag a BBMP seat for his wife, Vishwanath has a knack of pulling off victories in the last minute, like he did in 2019 General elections, where he was responsible for garnering over 75000 votes from his assembly segment.

Demography (registered voters)

  • Total Population – 3.88 lakhs
  • Men Voters – 1.99 lakhs
  • Women Voters – 1.89 lakhs

Caste arithmetic

  • Vokkaligas – 22%
  • Scheduled Caste – 15.2%
  • Scheduled Tribe – 2.84%
  • Muslims – 5.6%
  • Christians – 1.2%

2018 election result

S R Vishwanath scored a handsome victory over Hanumantegowda (JDS) by a margin of 42503 votes.

2. Byatarayanapura

This is a traditional Congress Bastion where in the ‘Ahinda’ (Minorities+ OBCs+ Dalits) factor has helped them sail through in the last three elections in addition to Vokkaligas backing them. The sitting MLA Krishna Byregowda is confident of another win despite struggling in 2018. This is because their main opposition, BJP has still not zeroed in on a candidate. Several names from A Ravi (brother of R Ashok), Munendra Kumar (close aide to B L Santosh) to Tammesha Gowda (close aide to Yedyurappa) have made rounds but the ticket is still up for grabs. The JDS has currently named a new candidate in Venugopal.

Looking at how things stand currently, the Congress hold 4 out of the 7 corporate wards while the remaining are with BJP. So, in order to turn this around, the BJP must perform like how they did in 2019 elections, securing over 1.3 lakh votes to INC’s 1.15 lakh. The Congress has a strong vote bank in the Vokkaligas and Kurubas besides Muslims whereas BJP are reliant on Lingayats, SC/ST and Upper caste votes with the 3rd placed JDS trying to cash in on the Vokkaligas. But for now, the chances are bleak for the BJP while Krishna Byregowda is all set to become the MLA for the 4th time.

Demography (registered voters)

  • Total Population – 4.48 lakhs
  • Men Voters – 2.33 lakhs
  • Women Voters – 2.15 lakhs

Caste arithmetic

  • Vokkaligas – 18%
  • Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe – 16.9%
  • Muslims – 13.7%
  • Kurubas – 6.9%
  • Lingayats – 2%
  • Others – 46%

2018 election result

Krishna Byregowda survived a scare over A Ravi (BJP) with a victory margin of 5671 votes.

3. K R Pura

The constituency was formed in 2008 after delimitation, with Nandish Reddy becoming its first MLA from the BJP. The next two assembly elections that followed 2008 saw Bhairati Basavaraj, who is often considered as the face of Kuruba community in the region, secure back-to-back victories for the Indian National congress. Interestingly, in the aftermath of congress MLAs crossing over to BJP in 2019, the by-polls held in KR Puram also gave a clear mandate to the incumbent leader. The Janata Dal have no real ground around this area.

Even though it looks like a real tussle between BJP and Congress, the BJP should step in quickly to sort the internal conflicts arising in the region. The corporator of KR Pura, Poornima Srinivas was a ticket aspirant from BJP but the party is looking to stick with the sitting MLA, leading to a cold war in the region between supporters of either leaders. This move might change the dynamics in the region, but Basavaraj is confident of pulling through with a strong charisma. The BJP holds 3 out of 7 corporation wards (Ramamurthy Nagar, Vijnanapura and K R Pura) while Congress have control over the other 4 centers.

Demography (registered voters)

  • Total Population – 4.79 lakhs
  • Men Voters – 2.51 lakhs
  • Women Voters – 2.28 lakhs

Caste arithmetic

  • Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe – 16.7%
  • Lingayats – 13.6%
  • Muslims – 8%
  • Brahmins – 5.9%
  • OBCs – 4%
  • Others – 51.8%

2019 by-poll election result

Bhairati Basavaraj secured a whooping 61.43% votes defeating his opponent by 63443 votes.

4. Yeshwantpura

The most awaited contest is in the Yeshwantpura constituency in North Bengaluru where the incumbent, ST Somshekhar is struggling to hold on to his lead at this moment. This might just be Janata Dal’s ‘KR Pete’ moment as they are trying to pull off a heist, with the inside support of Congress workers who are hell bent on defeating the BJP after their ex-leader jumped the ship in 2019. In addition to this, JDS Candidate T N Javarayi Gowda has been aggressive in his campaign and the party workers are confident of a comfortable victory this time around, after three failed attempts in the past.

Looking at the current scenario, the Congress is expected to field a weak candidate in Bhavana (actress) or Krishnam Raju to make sure that it is a direct contest between BJP and JDS. Given that it has significant rural votes (32.13%), Javarayi Gowda is a firm favorite to cash on, while ST Somshekhar is not someone who throws in the towel. The Vokkaliga leader, who is also the Mysuru incharge for BJP, has to dig in and woo his community voters, to secure a 4th straight win.

Demography (registered voters)

  • Total Population – 4.76 lakhs
  • Men Voters – 2.46 lakhs
  • Women Voters – 2.30 lakhs

Caste arithmetic

  • Vokkaligas – 34.9%
  • OBCs – 18.5%
  • Scheduled Caste/ Scheduled Tribe – 15.3%
  • Lingayats – 9.8%
  • Muslims – 7.6%
  • Christians – 0.9%
  • Others – 13%

2019 by-poll election result

ST Somashekhar (BJP) sailed through comfortably with a margin of 27699 votes over Javarayi Gowda (JDS).
