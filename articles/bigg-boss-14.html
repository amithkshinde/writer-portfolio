The mighty controversial show Bigg Boss, has entered the 14th Season and this time with a catchy phrase ‘Ab Scene Paltega’. The superstar of Bollywood Salman Khan hosts the show again and added that this show is going to be a befitting response to 2020, which has been a difficult year so far.

To give the housemates the pre-lockdown feel the makers have added a shopping mall, theatre, spa also to the sets of the Bigg Boss house. This season features, television couple Rubina Dilaik and Abhinav Shukla, actors Eijaz Khan, Jasmin Bhasin, Nishant Singh Malkhani, Pavitra Punia, singers Rahul Vaidya, Jaan Kumar, son of veteran singer Kumar Sanu – Jaan Kumar Sanu, Shehzad Deol, a model, singer-model Sara Gurpal and south actor Nikki Tamboli, actress Kavita Kaushik, actress and model Naina Singh, RJ Shardul Pandit, TV Actor Aly Goni. This season also features some of the older contestants of the previous seasons of the game namely Gauhar Khan from Bigg Boss 7, Hina Khan from Bigg Boss 11 and also Sidharth Shukla from Bigg Boss 13 who will be joining in as Seniors. The contestants had been tested and quarantined before letting them participate in the show.

The couple Rubina and Abhinav seem to be going against the captain of the house Kavita Kaushik. In one of the tasks given where in the captain was Kavita, she made the friends Jasmin Bhasin and Rubina Dilaik against each other and present their arguments against each other. Kavita even got in a war of words against Abhinav Shukla.

Contestant Rubina Dilaik had got a special advise from her fans, Salman Khan and also special guest Ekta Kapoor during the Weekend ka Vaar Episode. She was asked to follow her gut instinct and not let her husband Abhinav interfere or influence her. During one of the tasks related to Panchayat, Abhinav got agitated as his advise was falling on deaf ears, and she was also avoiding conversation with him. There seems to be a clear rift in the couple and this is being seen clearly as they are having arguments over small issues on the show.

The journey of the small screen bahu doesn’t seem to be going good on the show, she is insecure, has a superiority complex and is dominating. These are some of the things her fellow house mates have to say about her.

Rahul Vaidya called her a Shikshika in one of the fights and the name has got struck ever since. She started off her journey with as a power couple with her husband. The fans of Bigg Boss thought that being with her husband would give her the upper hand to sway decisions in their way and have more dominance. This seems to have gone and it seems as though the infighting of the couple is going to affect their own game. Rubina also has had irked the seniors and Salman Khan as well. The banter with Salman didn’t go pretty well and she was even told to leave the show

On Day 27 of the Bigg Boss, the Red Zone housemates Nikki Tamboli, Jaan Kumar Sanu, Rahul Vaidya and Pavitra Punia were given to call any other housemates from the green zone who they think should be in danger and then Eijaz Khan would have the final decision who would go to the red zone.

Nikki voted Kavita Kaushik to go to the Red Zone, Jaan Kumar Sanu put down his papers in the against Nishant Singh Malkani, Rahul Vaidya put up his camp against Jasmin Bhasin and Pavitra Punia voted against Rubina Dilaik.

This week we could see that 6 contestants were put up for voting by the public, the names of the 6 contestants are Rubina Dilaik, Rahul Vaidya, Jasmin Bhasin, Aly Gony, Abhinav Shukla and Pavitra Punia. The recent trends that are trickling in show that Rubina stands at the top with 34%, Rahul has managed to get 26%, followed by Jasmin at 21%, Abhinav at 9%, Aly at 7% and Pavitra with only 3%. If the trends go as is, then Pavitra may get evicted this week.
