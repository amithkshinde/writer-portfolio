The new dawn had rose in the chapter of the Indian History. India was independent from the clutches of the British Imperialism on 15th Aug 1947. Different factors were involved for us as a country to gain independence. In the aftermath of the Independence, India witnessed one of the most atrocious incidents in its post-independence history right after the day of gaining the freedom, the act of Partition from the British with backing of the Congress party, which involved the decisions of the great Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi and India’s first Prime Minister Jawahar Lal Nehru. This decision came after both Jinnah and Nehru wanted their own states of rule and was in no interest of any section of people in the society. This incident had a lot of repercussions with lakhs of people losing their houses and thousands of people being killed in the communal riots both in India and Pakistan, but unfortunately there were more Hindus on the receiving end on both the sides. MK Gandhiji went on a Satyagraha to take the situation under control. Even though he succeeded in doing it after several days of partition, the fire underneath against Gandhi in several packets across the country was left un-noticed. One such fire grew in the name of Nathuram Vinayak Godse.

The Nehru govt thought it had everything under the control, MK Gandhi the secular icon of our country was placed in the Birla House of Delhi where his daily chores and prayers were held and also the people gathered here in huge numbers. On 30th Jan 1948, the disciples of Gandhiji had gathered in large numbers for the multi-faith prayers that were organized every evening. The prayers began and Gandhiji moved towards the center stage, Nathuram Godse who was present in the huge gathering shrieked out and fired three bullets towards Gandhiji in point-blank range. Even in their wildest dreams had anyone expected or wanted this to happen, but one of the most unfortunate incidents in Indian history had taken place. The news broke out across the country and whole nation stood in a shock, yes it was their Independence hero who was assassinated and the encroachment of people was seen elevated across the country. This was termed as the first act of terrorism in the independent country and Nathuram Godse was labelled as the first terrorist of the country. Yes, this was an unfortunate and terrible incident and shouldn’t have happened, no one agrees upon this kind of violence. Nathuram Godse’s mindset to kill Gandhiji shouldn’t be accepted by anyone across the country. And, what happened after this incident from Gandhiji's disciples also shouldn't be accepted by anyone. The Congress left no time to blame the RSS and Hindu Mahasabha for the incident. People went on rampage against the RSS across the country, the offices were dethroned, people were hurt and also the women were harmed by the Non-violent warriors of the country. Amidst this outbreak, Congress was in full flow to take advantage of the situation to finish of their arch rivals and also their potential opposition once the General Elections were to be announced. The Anti-brahmin mindset kicked in at the right time for the Congress and result of which the grand old party planned for one of the most awful incidents against the Brahmins. On the other hand, Nathuram Godse knew that he would be bashed and even killed immediately after the mistake he was going to commit, but he had no clue about the biggest incident that was going to take place against his community. Yes, you read it right, against the community. The Congress planned and executed independent India’s first Pogrom (Massacre) against the Chitpavan Brahmins in the state of Maharashtra.

The New York Times Report

The New York Times had covered the Gandhi assassination episode in detail and had reported all the happenings in the country post the murder of Gandhiji. The attention to detail was pretty much evident when the then Prime Minister himself had to reach out to the press to stop the meticulous reporting of the assassination. Robert Trumbull, a reporter in the NY Times writes about the riots caused in Bombay on 31st Jan 1948 and to make a note this was the last article published in NY Times related to the riots, post Gandhiji’s murder.

Maureen Patterson Episode

After two decades to the Gandhiji’s assassination, Maureen Patterson a civil employee of the Federal Government of the United States visits India in research of the pogrom conducted against the Chitpavan Brahmins. Maureen Patterson has given a statement saying that she was denied the access to the files on the Gandhiji’s assassination by the then Indira Gandhi government. Maureen Patterson turns up in the ground and starts the research in multiple districts of Maharashtra. She concludes by saying it was not only restricted to the Chitpavan brahmins, but every brahmin community was tortured during this phase and she notifies the price of the atrocities maybe gauged at more than 12Cr Rupees in 1948.

Koenraad Elst, the author of “Gandhi & Godse” states in one of his interviews that if not Gandhi’s murder, there was no possible way for the Congress to come to power in the next general elections. Hindu Mahasabha, which showcased the Partition as the greatest act of cruelty, had gathered all the interests of the people against Gandhiji and Congress. But once the Congress started naming the Hindu Mahasabha as the murderers of Gandhiji, its popularity reduced and people started hating all the members of the Mahasabha and all of their loyalists moved to Congress. Also Dr. Elst adds at this point of time was the Nehruvian ideology sprouted and its aim to diminish the Hindus from the history and Brahmins in particular who were responsible for creation of RSS, Jansangh and many more nationalistic institutions. Even today we can see the same kind of hatred against the community by these Nehruvian ideologists and also from the less read section of the society, the Wokes.

The Brahmin Pogrom

The Chitpavan brahmins are one of the most notable communities in Maharashtra. The Peshwa under the Maratha Empire did belong to the same community. Freedom fighters like Bal Gangadhar Tilak, Gopala Krishna Gokhale, Vinayaka Damodar Savarkar and many more were Chitpavan Brahmins. Also, Nathuram Godse belonged to this section of the community. You know what is the worst possible act by a political party? The mileage it tries to gain over someone’s death. Congress party has done this over the years in its political structure, but the first act was in the early 1948 itself. After Gandhiji’s assassination, Congress carefully made the caste of the murderer public which led to the outrage against everyone of that community.

The day after Gandhiji’s assassination few of the riots broke out, several were killed and everyone in the neighborhood thought this would be a one-of-a-kind riot when a famous personality dies and ignored the whole episode. However, in the backdrop awaited the most dreadful acts in history of Independent India. Initially the atrocities were restricted to the major cities of Pune, Nagpur which was the headquarters of RSS and Mumbai. The news spread so fast that every brahmin community was attacked in their prominent region This fire against the brahmins spread to small districts of Satara, Kolhapur, Belagavi and its surroundings, where the brahmin population was in huge numbers.

In the smaller districts of Satara, Belagavi, Kolhapur the Chitpavan Brahmins were mostly owning most of the businesses and were in importance even in the government offices. Here in these districts, the houses, factories, offices of the Chitpavan Brahmins were listed and in the whole of February 1948 in every part of Maharashtra they were set ablaze and killed thousands of people. In one incident from Satara, a family consisting of 3-4 members with a surname of Godse were burnt live in a brutal act of loyalty for the Gandhian assassination. Many famous personalities had borne the violence for being a Chitpavan brahmin. Famous photographer, Bhaliji Pendharker’s studio was burnt into ashes by the rioters too.

The Congress in its usual terms created a false narrative against the freedom fighter Veer Savarkar, who was also one of the members of Hindu Mahasabha that he was seen distributing sweets as a celebration of Gandhiji’s demise. This took the outrage to a whole new level and more than 500+ Non-violence followers of Gandhiji were ready to perform the dreadful act of violence against Veer Savarkar and family. The Police however was successful in saving Savarkar from these Gandhiji ideologists but failed to save Veer Savarkar’s brother Narayan Savarkar from the rampage which led to his death. Later in a different scenario Savarkar was named as one of the accused in the Gandhiji assassination case, but he came clean under the court of law. Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru when asked about the violence going on Maharashtra, stated the government doesn’t know about it and even if there’s something going on as such will be a local issue and will be dealt by the district administration. Jawaharlal Nehru, thoughtfully took out this chapter from the history that he sold using the left inclined writers. Nehru was in all belief that if not taken today, this will backfire him and also his party in the future years to come.

Rest said, this will go down in history as one of the greatest acts of barbarism where the government gave free hand to the rioters to fulfill its political agenda. The Non-violence preached by Gandhi was thrown into dump, the next day of his death. Even today this mentality of Congress can be seen in several aspects of their party. If it was not Maureen Patterson and Koenraad Elst this section of history wouldn’t have come into mainstream. This is a lesson to several Indian historians and scholars to be brave and let open the history to the common people.
