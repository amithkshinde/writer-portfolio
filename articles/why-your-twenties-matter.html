Defining Decade as the name suggests is that decade of human life which perhaps goes on to define your personality and even goes on to define how one should make decisions that impact the next upcoming decades. It does not matter whether it is a boy or a girl, the people in the age groups of 20 to 30 years undergo a transformation that will lead to significant changes in their life.

Twentysomething’s as author quotes throughout the book has a huge significance in the society. The author quotes many real-life scenarios where the Twentysomething’s have suffered significantly due to the choices that they made during their twenties.

MEG JAY, who is a clinical psychologist and an associate professor of education at the University of Virginia, has authored this book. Every Twentysomething who reads this book sees him-or herself in the stories which are included in the book. The book starts with quotes of some studies on lifespan development and research which examined dozens of life stories, written by prominent, successful people toward the end of their lives; this study focuses on the circumstances and people that had the strongest influence on how life unfolded thereafter. While important events took place from birth until death, those that determined the years ahead were most heavily concentrated during the twentysomething years.

During this period, people in Twenties leave home or college and become more independent. There is a burst of self-creation, a time when, what they do determines who they will become.

In the thirties, consequential experiences start to slow. School will be over or nearly so. People would have invested time in careers or made the choice not to. They, or their friends, may be in relationships and starting families. They may own homes or have other responsibilities that make it difficult to change directions.

With about 80 percent of life’s most significant events taking place by age thirty-five, people in thirtysomethings and beyond largely either continue with, or correct for, the moves that would have been made during their twenty something years.

The deceptive irony is that our twenty something years may not feel all that consequential. It is easy to imagine that life’s significant experiences begin with big moments and exciting encounters, but this is not how it happens. Researchers in the same study found that most of the substantial and lasting events like those that led to career success, family fortune, personal bliss, etc.. developed across days or weeks or months with little immediate dramatic effect. The importance of these experiences was not necessarily clear at the time but, in retrospect, the subjects recognized that these events had sharply defined their futures

To a great extent, our lives are decided by far-reaching twentysomething moments we may not realize are happening at all. This book is about recognizing those defining twentysomething moments. It is about why your twenties matter, and how to make the most of them now.

Takeaways from the book are categorized through:

  • Work
  • Love
  • Body

Work. In this section Meg talks about how to create the identity capital, how to make “weak ties” that potentially can lead to job making opportunities. She also goes on and educates about the negative impact of the social media that we try to portray. The impact Facebook has led to humans creating a customized life, pros, and cons of these are discussed in this section.

Love. Meg quotes examples of some of her clients having troubles in building a family and even some examples where her clients were facing major issues and how they responded to those scenarios. Dating randomly, having one night stands and the consequences that follow. Meg at the end of this section shows how can we make wise decisions and take ownership for these decisions

Body. In this Meg quotes facts and figures that reveal quantitative data about the body and how one needs to plan the budgeting and even train the brain. She also speaks about the fertility aspect of the human body and why it is important to make wise choices at times and not keep extending the plans unnecessarily. Meg gives a very practical solutions as to how we should respect our body and then plan our future.
