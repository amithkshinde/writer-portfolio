After the ground report from Kittur Karnataka, let us travel to the south of Karnataka covering the districts of Chikkaballapura and Kolara. Dominated by Vokkaliga and Kuruba community, the population is mainly dependent on Agriculture and Industrial chores. Even after 75 years of Independence both the districts suffer from the shortage of basic amenities like Drinking Water and proper housing facilities. With the dream of “Ettinahole” project yet to come true, the districts go for vote once again in a 3-way tussle between the national parties and a regional party. Once considered as the bastion of the JDS, the party today is finding difficult to gain the trust of the people due to several decisions taken from the top brass, which had benefits only for the districts of their interest. The Chikkaballapur and Kolara district combined contains 10 seats out of which the Congress sits on top with 8 seats followed by JDS having 2 seats with both the BJP and Independent candidates having a seat each. Let’s dive in to get some detailed analysis of all the constituencies.

Chikkaballapura

The fortress of the Congress party here faces primary opposition from the Independents in most of the constituencies. In the upcoming 2023 elections, the independent candidates have an edge in few constituencies to make a grand entry to Vidhana Soudha. The constituencies of Bagepalli and Gauribidanur is going to see a neck to neck to fight between the Congress party and the Independents. As of today, in Gauribidanur the independent candidate Puttaswamy Gowda is slightly ahead of the current MLA Shivashankar Reddy. The presence of communist party, independents and also the district president of BJP Ramalingappa, likely to contest from the same seat, Bagepalli will witness a poll for the ages to come. Here, in the multi-way battle CPI(M) might emerge as the winner against all the odds. The Shidlaghatta and Chintamani constituencies will again see a straight battle between the Congress and JDS in the 2023 elections. In Shidlaghatta, due to an internal fight among several aspirants from the Congress party along with the Current MLA V. Muniyappa, JDS will have an upper hand in the constituency. Chintamani, which has always vouched for simple and clean politics, will go for polls this election with the same old pattern. The margins in the past elections are always been barely a few thousand votes, by which the parties will find it tough to sense the mood of the voters. With BJP trying to make inroads in the constituency, will be a major threat to the current MLA.

Kolara

The district of Kolar has always voted for the stalwarts like 7-time MP K H Muniyappa, ex-speaker Ramesh Kumar, ex-minister Krishnaiah Shetty and several others. The huge inclusion to the above list was about to happen with ex-CM Siddaramaiah coming into the fray. The Congress party considered as the torch bearers of the prominent Kuruba community, expects to vote them in the same manner as the previous elections. The JDS with its new Pancharatna programs has made a noticeable impact in the district. It is likely to increase the MLA count from 1 to 2. The mere presence of BJP here isn’t going to impact any results in the district. The constituencies of Mulabagalu and Malur has never let the saffron party to gain any kind of political mileage in the scene. The Straight battle between the Congress and JDS, will be seen in the upcoming elections too. Sammruddhi Manjunath is likely to have an upper hand in Mulabagalu against the current MLA H Nagesh. With BJP’s Malur Krishnaiah Shetty out of the electoral politics, both the Congress and JDS are up against each other in a two-way battle. The Malur constituency after the 2008 elections, has voted for JDS and Congress once each in 2013 and 2018 respectively. Known for his social service and availability, JDS candidate Ramegowda here might emerge as the winner against the current MLA H Y Nanjegowda. Let’s see the two reserved constituencies from the district, KGF and Bangarpete. KGF in particular was actually a bastion of BJP with Y.Sampangi and Ramakka winning consecutively in 2008 and 2013 respectively. The voters today in KGF has zero hope on BJP, thanks to the corruption cases against both of their previous MLAs. Congress sits firmly on top with the current MLA Roopakala actively working in the constituency and is also likely to retain the seat. Bangarpete is again the house for old school politics of the region, where the faceoff is between the so-called messiahs of minorities, Congress and JDS. Current MLA S N Narayanaswamy will have a tough fight against the JDS Candidate Mallesh Babu, who is in a hope for the emotional wave of losing from past few elections to work for him.

Constituencies to watch out for

Chikkaballapura (taluk)

The Man on mission during the Covid, once the Congress party’s poster boy, currently serving as the health minister in the state, Dr. K Sudhakar will test his luck again from the constituency of Chikkaballapura. K Sudhakar defected the congress party in 2019 and joined BJP, which is considered as the boon for the latter. There was no presence of BJP in the whole district until the inclusion of K Sudhakar was made. He will be up against the ex-MLA K P Bacchegowda of the JDS, who is working round the clock to defeat the BJP candidate. Multiple efforts from the congress party were made to bring back their old knight. But the highly aspirant minister sensed bigger opportunities in BJP, which has only a few notable Vokkaliga leaders. By presenting some of the notable programs from the central government, K Sudhakar is likely to make his place better in the district and also in the state politics. The Congress party is yet to find a proper replacement for Sudhakar in the constituency. This seat of Chikkaballapura will see a politics of a different nature this time, where both the Congress and JDS might combine together to form a single force against Dr. K Sudhakar. But having said that, Charisma and the developmental works of K Sudhakar will always put him in a better place compared to the previous elections.

Kolara (taluk)

The stage was set, the lights were lit to witness one of the greatest battles among the Kuruba community itself. Ex-CM Siddaramaiah who was on the run across the state to find winnable constituency for his name, dropped his foot and made a bold statement to contest from Kolara. The loss of Chamundeshwari in 2018 and the win in Badami by the barest of margins against B Sriramulu has positioned the ex-CM in a sense of trouble. This trouble was not only for Sidda himself, but also for the party. Portrayed as the eligible CM candidate, Sidda chose Kolar as his mode of transport to the Vidhana Sabha. Multiple Surveys conducted, several leaders campaigned, even Siddaramaiah himself went to Kolar and announced his candidature along with the other stalwarts of the district. But the High Command was in a different mood and pitched the ex-CM in a constituency where he could win without any major threats offered. Varthur Prakash from the BJP, who was once seen in the close circles of Siddaramaiah was up against his own Guru and was believed to be a major hurdle for the ex-CM’s win. As of today, Congress is yet to announce their candidate whereas JDS and the BJP have fielded CMR Srinath and Varthur Prakash respectively. The huge population of minorities turns out to be a gamechanger in Kolar. Current MLA K Srinivas Gowda has vacated his seat and is in search of a new home. Even if the ex-CM’s candidature was announced from the constituency, JDS is likely to win with the Kuruba community votes being split between Siddaramaiah and Varthur Prakash and the major factor which they bank on is the support from Vokkaliga community and minorities.

Srinivaspura

The constituency which had a tradition of selecting alternate candidates from Congress and JDS, broke all the norms in 2018 elections by re-electing the Congress candidate Ramesh Kumar. Ramesh Kumar, who hails from the Brahmin community doesn't bank on the caste-based politics which the district is known for. Ramesh Kumar known for his clever articulations and excellent deliverance served as the Speaker of Karnataka Legislative Assembly from 2013-2018 under the Siddharamaiah’s leadership. The Ex-speaker will lock horns against the JDS candidate and ex-MLA Venkatashiva Reddy. The JDS candidate banks on all the caste-based factors offering him a secure place in the constituency. The backlash among the multiple groups and with K H Muniyappa’s aide standing right against the Ex-speaker, he’ll have to face the elections in an isolated phase. BJP is fielding Dr. Venugopal, who also will be capturing the votes from Ramesh Kumar’s kitty. As of today, Venkatashiva Reddy is all set to return to power from the constituency of Srinivaspura.
